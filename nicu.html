About NICU Care in Bhiwandi

The Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) is a specialist environment for newborns who need close observation, supportive treatment, and a carefully monitored recovery setting. At Alexis Hospital, Bhiwandi, our NICU in Bhiwandi provides focused care for premature babies, low birth weight infants, and newborns with respiratory, feeding, or metabolic concerns. The goal is to support stability, promote safe growth, and provide parents with informed medical guidance as their baby recovers. This level of care is designed to support early recovery in a safe, supervised setting.

Babies Who May Need NICU Care

Premature babies
Low birth weight infants
Respiratory distress or low oxygen
Temperature instability
Feeding or dehydration concerns
Medical or congenital complications

Family Support & Follow-Up

  • Premature delivery or very low birth weight
  • Breathing difficulty, apnea, or low oxygen
  • Poor feeding, dehydration, or jaundice
  • Temperature instability or low blood sugar
  • Need for close observation following birth or surgery
  • Ongoing neonatal review and guided discharge planning
