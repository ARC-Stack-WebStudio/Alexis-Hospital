About Kidney Care in Bhiwandi

Healthy kidneys play an essential role in filtering waste, maintaining fluid balance, controlling blood pressure, and supporting overall body function. At Alexis Hospital, Bhiwandi, our Kidney Disease Care in Bhiwandi focuses on early identification of renal problems, careful evaluation, and ongoing management of conditions affecting kidney health. Patients with diabetes, high blood pressure, recurrent urinary issues, or long-standing symptoms may need regular review to monitor kidney function and reduce risk. Dr Ganesh Ahire, M.B.B.S., M.D. (Medicine) helps provide medically guided care for patients seeking a trusted Kidney Doctor in Bhiwandi.

Common Kidney Health Concerns

Swelling in feet, hands, or face
Changes in urination pattern
Blood in urine or foamy urine
Persistent fatigue and weakness
High blood pressure or kidney-related concerns
Unexplained nausea or loss of appetite

When to Consult a Doctor

  • Swelling, puffiness, or sudden weight gain
  • Reduced urine output or changes in urination
  • Pain in the lower back or flank area
  • Blood in urine or persistent frothy urine
  • Long-standing high blood pressure or diabetes with worsening symptoms
  • Unexplained fatigue, nausea, or poor appetite
