About General Medicine in Bhiwandi

General medicine, also called internal medicine, covers the diagnosis and management of a wide range of adult health concerns. At Alexis Hospital, Bhiwandi, our General Physician in Bhiwandi care focuses on common and complex medical issues, from acute illnesses and fever to chronic conditions such as diabetes, thyroid disorders, high blood pressure, and long-term wellness management. This care model is designed to provide clear medical guidance, early diagnosis, and continuous support for patients seeking a dependable MD Doctor in Bhiwandi for everyday health concerns.

When to Consult

  • Fever, weakness, or persistent tiredness
  • Ongoing blood pressure or sugar concerns
  • Unexplained weight changes, appetite loss, or frequent illness
  • Need for routine health check-up and medical follow-up
  • Symptoms that are recurring or affecting daily life
  • Preventive care and long-term management guidance
