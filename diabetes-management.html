About Diabetes Management

Diabetes is a chronic metabolic condition that requires continuous care and attention. At Alexis Hospital, our dedicated team provides comprehensive diabetes management including regular blood sugar monitoring, lifestyle modification guidance, and personalised treatment plans. We focus on preventive care to reduce the risk of complications, offer dietary counselling, and ensure long-term management strategies that empower patients to take control of their health and lead productive, fulfilling lives.

Common Symptoms

Frequent urination
Excessive thirst
Fatigue
Blurred vision
Slow wound healing
Weight changes

When to Consult a Doctor

  • Persistent high blood sugar
  • Sudden weight loss
  • Frequent infections
  • Dizziness
  • Vision changes
